1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, improve performance, enhance security, and provide a better browsing experience.

Cookies do not typically contain information that directly identifies you, but they may be linked to information associated with your account or device.

2. Why We Use Cookies

CareerSync uses cookies to:

  • Keep you signed in securely.
  • Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Improve website speed and performance.
  • Understand how visitors use our platform.
  • Personalize your browsing experience.
  • Enhance security and detect suspicious activity.
  • Improve resume builder, AI tools, and career resources.
  • Measure website traffic and performance.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function properly. Without them, features such as login, account security, and resume creation may not work correctly.

Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with CareerSync by collecting anonymous usage data to improve performance and user experience.

Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences, such as language, theme, and recently viewed pages, providing a more personalized experience.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us measure visitor activity, page performance, and overall website usage so we can continuously improve our services.

Marketing Cookies

With your permission, marketing cookies may be used to show relevant updates, promotions, and career-related recommendations. You can choose to disable these cookies at any time.

6. How Long Cookies Are Stored

Some cookies are temporary and are automatically removed when you close your browser. These are commonly called session cookies.

Others remain on your device for a limited period to remember your preferences for future visits. These are often called persistent cookies.

The duration depends on the purpose of each cookie and your browser settings.
