    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Got questions? We have answers. Find everything you need to know about CareerSync.

    How do I apply for a job on CareerSync?
    Simply create a free account, complete your profile, upload your resume, and click "Apply" on any job listing. Our AI will match you with the best-fit opportunities automatically. You can also apply via WhatsApp for supported roles.
    Is CareerSync completely free for candidates?
    Yes! CareerSync is 100% free for all candidates. Job search, resume builder, AI career tools, interview prep — all free. We only charge employers who post jobs and access candidate profiles.
    Can freshers and 0-experience candidates apply?
    Absolutely! We have thousands of fresher-friendly roles across BPO, sales, customer support, marketing, and IT. Our AI career coach also helps freshers build their profile from scratch and land their first job faster.
    How does the AI job matching work?
    Our AI analyzes your resume, skills, experience, location preference, and salary expectations. It then scores and ranks available jobs by relevance, showing you the best-fit opportunities with a match percentage so you apply smarter, not more.
    How long does the hiring process take?
    Our verified employer partners commit to responding within 24–72 hours of a valid application. From first application to offer letter, most candidates complete the process in 5–15 working days depending on the role and company.
    Can employers contact me directly?
    Yes, verified employers can contact you if you have enabled "Open to Opportunities" in your profile settings. You control your privacy — you can restrict visibility to specific industries or hide your profile at any time.
    How do I upload my resume?
    After logging in, go to your profile and click "Upload Resume". You can upload PDF or Word formats up to 5MB. Our AI will instantly parse, score, and suggest improvements to maximize your ATS compatibility.
    Can I edit my profile after signing up?
    Yes, you can edit your profile anytime from your dashboard. Update your skills, experience, preferred location, expected salary, and resume as often as needed. Keeping your profile updated improves your match quality significantly.

    Freshers & Experienced Welcome

    Both freshers and experienced professionals can apply. We provide training and support for career development.

    Terms & Conditions
    Fresher Work Mode — Office Only

    Freshers (0 months experience) are required to work from the office. This ensures proper training, mentorship, and supervision during the initial phase of employment. Office attendance is mandatory.

    Experienced Work Mode — Work From Home Available

    Experienced professionals with 3+ months of experience may be eligible for Work From Home options in select companies and departments. This is subject to company approval, role requirements, and performance evaluation.

    Performance & Attendance Requirements

    Regardless of work mode, employees are expected to maintain good attendance records and consistent performance. Regular evaluations will determine eligibility for work flexibility options.

    Work Location & Travel

    Employees may be required to travel to office or client locations for training, meetings, or special projects as per company requirements. Travel expenses will be reimbursed as per company policy.

    Work Hours & Availability

    Standard office hours apply (9 AM - 6 PM with 1 hour lunch break). Work From Home employees must remain available during working hours and maintain regular communication with the team.

    Policy Modifications

    The company reserves the right to modify work mode policies, location requirements, or employment terms based on business needs. Employees will be notified of any significant changes in advance.

    Confidentiality & Professional Conduct

    All employees must maintain professional conduct and confidentiality of company and client information at all times, whether working from office or home. Breach of confidentiality may result in disciplinary action.

    Equipment & Technology

    Company-provided equipment and devices are for official use only. Employees working from home are responsible for maintaining secure internet connectivity and protecting company data.

    Transition & Experience Verification

    After 3+ months of service and successful performance reviews, freshers may request to transition to Work From Home. Previous work experience must be verified through original documents before claiming experienced category benefits.
