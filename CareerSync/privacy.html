1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal, resume, account, and technical information when you use CareerSync.

  • Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number
  • Resume Information, Account Details, Profile Information
  • Browser Type, Device Information, Operating System, IP Address
  • Pages Visited, Session Duration, Referral Source
  • Payment Information

If you purchase premium services, payments are processed through secure third-party payment providers. CareerSync does not store your complete payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide resume-building and career services.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Personalize recommendations and content.
  • Process payments securely.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Send important account updates.
  • Send promotional emails only if you choose to receive them.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

CareerSync uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, improve performance, analyze visitor behavior, enhance security, and provide a smoother browsing experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, although some features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to help protect your information, including encryption, secure servers, and access controls.

While we strive to protect your personal data, no method of online transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing of Information

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal information.

  • With trusted service providers who help operate our platform.
  • With secure payment processors for transaction processing.
  • When required by applicable laws or legal authorities.
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of CareerSync and its users.

6. Your Privacy Rights

  • Access your personal information.
  • Update or correct your information.
  • Request deletion of your account or personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications.
  • Request a copy of your stored information, where applicable.

To exercise these rights, please contact our support team.

7. Third-Party Services

CareerSync may include links to third-party websites, tools, or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, improve platform performance, and maintain account security.

When your data is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

9. Children's Privacy

CareerSync is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If such information is identified, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements, or business practices. Continued use of CareerSync after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
